Sumitomo Metal Mining (SMM) needed to commercialise CWO® (caesium-doped tungsten oxide), a near-infrared (NIR) absorbing nanoparticle facing patent expiry and limited market traction. Veridane Studio, the firm's creative arm, led brand strategy, audience segmentation and sector-specific go-to-market, rebranding it as SOLAMENT™. A consumer-facing proof of concept, the DOWN-LESS DOWN JACKET (a solar-reactive garment), made the value proposition tangible for B2B audiences. Results: 500% increase in business leads, 30+ leads from a single event and 5+ international design awards, including Red Dot. Applications are expanding into heat-mitigating workwear and agricultural shade nets.
