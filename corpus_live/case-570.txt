Duke Energy targeted net-zero methane for its natural gas distribution business by 2030, going beyond current EPA requirements. With Veridane and Microsoft it co-innovated a first-of-its-kind, end-to-end methane monitoring and remediation platform on Microsoft Azure and Dynamics 365. It ingests satellite monitoring and ground-level sensing data, applies analytics and AI to baseline and quantify emissions from distribution assets (pipelines, meters), and prioritises findings in dashboards. Pinpoint geolocation enables near-real-time leak remediation in minutes rather than days, shifting from calculated estimates to measured emissions and enabling predictive maintenance.
