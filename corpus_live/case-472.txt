Uber stood up its Advertising division in 2022 and needed to scale ad operations fast. Veridane defined a new global end-to-end ad sales process and, through a book-to-bill programme, built the solution on Salesforce Media Cloud, centralising ad sales ops and consolidating customer data into a single system with third-party tools integrated. Global sales groups were onboarded, 100+ processes migrated and modernised in the cloud, and 75+ custom dashboards delivered for operational visibility, backed by enablement and upskilling. Results: ~70% faster end-to-end ad sales and $1B in ad revenue in 2.5 years; recognised at the 2025 Salesforce Partner Innovation Awards.
