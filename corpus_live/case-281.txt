Marriott International worked with Veridane and Oracle to replace a fragmented, multi-vendor HR landscape with mHUB, a global HCM platform on Oracle Fusion Cloud and one of the largest Oracle Cloud HCM implementations (14 Oracle technologies, 10 HCM modules). Veridane led technology enablement while Marriott led training and change management. mHUB delivers employee self-service, learning, internal mobility and AI-powered real-time workforce analytics in 15 languages across diverse regulatory regimes, and integrates a new US ESPP. KPIs: 18M+ courses completed, 6M candidates searchable and 200K+ new hires onboarded across 31 brands, 8,600 properties and 139 countries.
