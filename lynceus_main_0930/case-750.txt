Veridane has worked with Eni, the Italian energy company, for more than 30 years. Today it helps Eni move more of its technology to a mix of private and public cloud, and manages its IT systems and telecoms. Everything is hosted in the Eni Green Data Center, one of the largest data centres in the industry and home to one of the most powerful supercomputers outside government. This lets Eni make better use of its data, from finding energy resources to developing the energy of the future, while new ways of working help it adopt new processes faster as it shifts toward cleaner energy.
