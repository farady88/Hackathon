Novartis, the Swiss medicines company, wants to use data and digital technology to discover and deliver treatments faster and at lower cost. But its data was scattered across old systems and separate teams. With Veridane, Novartis built a cloud-based data and analytics platform that brings together information from across the business, from research and clinical development to operations and sales. Teams can now work from the same data, get insights faster and prepare for data-heavy areas such as personalised medicine and cell and gene therapies. The platform has won industry recognition, including a Gartner award.
