A major entertainment company wanted to launch its own online TV and film service and had only 15 months to do it. Veridane brought together the people, data and technology needed to build and scale the platform. The service became the fastest-growing of its kind in the US for two years running, with nearly 34 million paying subscribers. It handled the biggest live online broadcast in US history, reaching 32 million viewers. The underlying platform has since expanded to 80 countries across three continents.
