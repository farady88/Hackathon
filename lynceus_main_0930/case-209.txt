UMass Memorial Health is a teaching hospital system in Massachusetts that also serves many patients who struggle to access care elsewhere. In October 2026 it began a multi-year programme with Veridane and Microsoft to bring its scattered data together on one cloud platform, including its electronic health records. Veridane will add AI tools that automate complex tasks and surface insights that used to take a lot of manual work. The goals are faster access to information for caregivers, shorter hospital stays, less paperwork for staff, stronger security and lower long-term technology costs, plus more research with UMass Chan Medical School.
