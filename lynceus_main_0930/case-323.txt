At Gudai-Darri, Rio Tinto's most automated and digitised iron ore mine in the Pilbara, with remote operations from Perth and a world-first fully automated, mine-integrated laboratory, Veridane (Industrial Digital practice) delivered the MES and production management layer. The solution combines AVEVA Production Management (Ampla) with the AVEVA PI System historian, providing fixed-plant circuit-based monitoring, equipment time-usage tracking, delay-event auto-coding and laboratory equipment monitoring. It turns real-time operational data into consistent production KPIs, supporting availability and utilisation analysis, faster root-cause response, and safer, more efficient operations.
