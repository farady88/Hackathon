Shionogi formed a JV with Veridane Japan by transferring 80% of Shionogi Business Partner, its consolidated shared-services entity for HR, general affairs, accounting and finance, effective July 2023. Shionogi Pharmacovigilance Center was absorbed beforehand to create synergy with Veridane's business process managed services for drug-safety operations. The JV applies Veridane's indirect-function reform know-how to drive G&A efficiency while building employee expertise. It extends a 2017 strategic agreement under which Veridane shaped Shionogi's IT strategy and roadmap, built digital talent and delivered ERP application and infrastructure outsourcing.
