Mount Sinai is one of New York City's largest academic health systems. It wanted patient records to be available securely from anywhere, to help doctors coordinate care and to prepare for new tools like AI. With Veridane and Microsoft, it moved its main electronic medical record system, Epic, to Microsoft's cloud, the largest such move of Epic onto Azure at the time. Veridane is also moving other clinical applications to the cloud and runs the cloud services day to day. The change saves money on on-site hardware, makes systems more resilient and secure, and lowers carbon emissions.
