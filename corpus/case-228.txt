Repsol joined the Copilot for Microsoft 365 early access program and, with Veridane and Microsoft, was among the first enterprises to deploy Copilot organisation-wide. Use cases include meeting recaps with automated extraction of tasks, owners and deadlines; Word-to-PowerPoint content generation; and RFP analysis and authoring in finance. A 550-employee pilot ran four months of experimentation and measurement across efficiency, quality and employee experience, backed by an integrated adoption programme of demos, hands-on training, gamification and a Community of Practice. As published in MIT Technology Review: average time savings of 121 minutes per employee per week.
