Veridane has been Vodafone's long-term reinvention partner. The work includes a cloud-first transformation, a global finance overhaul on SAP (one of SAP's largest migrations), cybersecurity, and automated self-service and intelligent care. It also includes a GenAI chatbot for the VOXI youth brand. Veridane is now helping Vodafone commercialise VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions), its shared services centre covering network monitoring, HR, technology services, finance and contact-centre operations. The aim is to sell this as a service to other telcos, which creates new revenue and drives operational efficiency and better customer experience
