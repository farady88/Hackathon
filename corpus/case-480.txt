Leonardo's Helicopter Division needed to replace paper-based, manual aftersales ordering and a platform covering only a subset of services. Veridane, with SAP and Microsoft, led design-to-implementation of an omnichannel CX ecosystem of two synergistic systems, including a customer portal on Microsoft Dynamics 365 as the single entry point to aftersales: service ticketing, technical support, interactive dashboards, video tutorials, business accounts, a publications library and asset management (flight hours, availability). Available 24/7 on desktop and mobile, the best-of-breed platform is built to scale across all Leonardo divisions and is lifting customer satisfaction.
