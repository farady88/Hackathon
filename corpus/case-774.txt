Post-acute provider PruittHealth saw skilled nursing facility (SNF) revenue fall ~$75M amid COVID-19 labour shortages, admission restrictions and inflation-driven margin pressure. Over 18 months, Veridane applied a data-centric methodology across three growth levers: sales effectiveness, cost optimisation, and talent acquisition and retention, redesigning recruitment and retention programmes for RNs and CNAs amid federal vaccine mandates and wage pressure. Results versus engagement baseline: +4.8% revenue growth and +4.0% contribution margin growth, outpacing the industry and expanding access to post-acute care for seniors.
