Best Buy partnered with Veridane and Google to deploy gen AI across customer care. A gen AI virtual assistant on BestBuy.com, the app and the contact centre enables self-service for product troubleshooting, order delivery and scheduling changes, and Geek Squad subscription and My Best Buy Memberships management, with seamless escalation to live agents. Agent-assist tools analyse conversations in real time to surface next-best recommendations, detect sentiment, mine call data to prevent repeat issues and automate knowledge-base navigation, reducing handling effort and freeing agents for empathetic, high-value interactions.
