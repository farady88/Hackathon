Belden has long made the cables, switches and other equipment that connect factories and warehouses. It wanted to move beyond selling hardware and offer software that helps customers run those networks more intelligently. Veridane helped Belden build Belden Horizon, a platform that collects data from machines and network devices on the factory floor and uses AI to turn it into real-time insights. Customers can manage their networks remotely and spot problems sooner. For Belden, it opens new revenue streams and a closer, ongoing relationship with customers, with an ambition for 20% of its business to come from solutions by 2028.
