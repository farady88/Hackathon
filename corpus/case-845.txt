CBA, which supports ~40% of Australia's payments, replaced its legacy core rather than extend it. With Veridane, it built a cloud-based core on SAP Transaction Banking (TRBK) on AWS, covering balances, payments and regulatory reporting. Delivery used controlled, incremental change instead of a high-risk big-bang cutover, built-in automation and rapid-recovery safeguards, and clear end-to-end service ownership with event streaming for real-time visibility, so new capabilities ship in hours rather than months. Cutover landed with minimal customer disruption at 70M+ daily transactions. Outcomes: ~30% faster performance and ~30% lower daily run costs.
