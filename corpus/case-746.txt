DG MARE (European Commission) is migrating siloed fisheries applications, covering vessel geolocation and catch declarations from 27 EU member states and third countries, to AWS and consolidating them into a unified data platform, Ocean Store. The legacy business rule engine runs 2,000+ data-validation rules that needed refactoring for cloud-native services, an estimated 12,000 person-days manually. Veridane's Public Service Data & AI Studio built an LLM-based code-generation tool, grounded in DG MARE's existing codebase and documentation, that refactors each rule in ~1 minute at ~90% accuracy with human-in-the-loop validation, accelerating analytics for IUU fishing enforcement.
