BBVA, a Spanish bank founded in 1857, wanted to serve customers wherever they are, which today mostly means on their phones. Working with Veridane, it built a new way of selling digitally, backed by investment in cloud, data and AI. Opening an account now takes minutes rather than days, using facial recognition in the mobile app and real-time checks to spot fraud. Bank-wide data helps BBVA understand what each customer is likely to need next. More than 62 million customers now use its digital channels, almost eight in ten sales happen digitally, and in 2025 it won 11.5 million new customers and posted a record €10.11 billion profit.
