After the 2021 merger, VMO2 had siloed operations and legacy tech stacks. Veridane helped it modernise its digital core and make CX central to the operating model. The work included a cloud-based digital core that unifies omnichannel customer data, plus Amazon Connect as an AI-powered contact centre. Together they drive first-contact resolution with no agent transfers, and real-time AI agent assist suggests next best responses. Results include an NPS uplift of up to 35 points in some service areas and 89% same-day complaint closure.
