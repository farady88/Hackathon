Microsoft is building and upgrading data centres at huge speed to keep up with demand for cloud and AI services. Keeping track of the servers, network equipment and spare parts flowing to those sites across many countries is a major challenge. Veridane helped Microsoft redesign how it plans and manages this supply chain. A new shared data model halved the number of hardware product codes and removed dozens of manual processes. Two "control towers" now track equipment in transit and steer it to the data centres that need capacity most. The programme has delivered $100 million in cost savings.
