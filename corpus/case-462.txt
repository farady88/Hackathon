EDF is one of the UK's largest energy suppliers. To build a simpler business and help customers make better energy choices, it is moving its customers onto Kraken, the technology platform behind Octopus Energy. Veridane and Kraken Technologies are working alongside EDF's own teams to speed up the move, and more than five million electricity and gas meter points have already been migrated. Kraken uses data and AI to bring customer and operational information together, enabling things like cheaper prices when renewable energy is plentiful, better service, and lower costs for the supplier.
