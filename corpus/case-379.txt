Carlsberg, the Danish brewer with more than 140 beer brands, was considering a joint venture between its UK business and an independent UK brewer and pub operator. The two companies worked very differently: one sold mostly through shops using an outside delivery firm, the other mainly through its own pubs and delivery network. Combining their systems could have derailed the deal. Veridane created a clear plan showing what the merger would involve and cost, grounded in its experience running Carlsberg's systems. It then worked with both companies to bring their operations together, cutting time and cost and setting the venture up to grow.
